A creative team that works the way Spotify does

SketchDeck is a creative studio. We design presentations, brand systems, video, illustration, social content, infographics and much more. We've worked with Spotify teams since 2018, across six accounts and more than 350 projects.

REVERB and how the membership works

One monthly pool of credits, used as and when you need it

REVERB is Spotify's membership with SketchDeck. Each month a set number of credits goes into your credit pool. Every request draws from the credit pool, so small jobs use a few and larger projects use more. Credits you haven't used roll over for 60 days.

From brief to final files in four steps

  1. 1Brief

    You send a request with what you need, the audience and the deadline, or add us as the Vendor in your Airtable setup

  2. 2Estimate

    We confirm the scope and estimated credits it will use before anything starts.

  3. 3Design and feedback

    A first round comes back to you within 1-3 business days, depending on the urgency.

  4. 4Delivery

    Final files in the formats you need, ready to use.

Typical turnaround

  • Small jobssuch as a slide or a social asset1-2 working days

  • Larger projectssuch as a branding system or a videousually runs ~3 weeks total

How Spotify teams have used the membership this year

Between October 2025 and October 2026, Spotify teams used around $167,750 in credits, an average of about $13,750 a month. The busiest months were December, April and July.
