AI Show & Tell

A deck template for an internal AI show-and-tell series, with a pixel-pattern identity that animates.

Year
2026
Services
Deck template, Event identity, Motion cover

The brief

Spotify runs internal sessions where teams demo what they are building with AI. They needed a shared template so every presenter looks like part of the same event, plus a cover that could open the session on screen.

The idea

A pixelated pattern in red, electric blue and lime, shifting like a low-resolution signal, frames white content panels with a clipped corner. The AIM mark sits in the corner of every slide. The same pattern animates in the cover video, so the identity moves before anyone speaks.

What we made

Two rounds of style discovery and three rounds of full design. The template includes three cover variants, agenda, chapter slides, headline, one- and two-column text, three-up layouts, image slides and a closing slide, plus the animated cover.
