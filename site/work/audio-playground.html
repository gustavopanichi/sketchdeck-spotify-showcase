REVERB and how the membership works

One monthly pool of credits, used as and when you need it

REVERB is Spotify's membership with SketchDeck. Each month a set number of credits goes into your credit pool. Every request draws from the credit pool, so small jobs use a few and larger projects use more. Credits you haven't used roll over for 60 days.

Branding · 2025

Audio Playground

An event identity for Spotify's family day, built to be loud, playful and endlessly recombinable across signage, screens and merch.

Year
2025
Services
Event identity, Logo system, Illustration, Signage & collateral, Motion, Brand book

The brief

Spotify needed a visual identity for Audio Playground, an internal event that turns the office into a space for families and kids. The identity had to feel unmistakably Spotify yet entirely its own, work on a lobby screen as well as on a hot-dog menu, and ship in time for a day at the London Adelphi building.

The idea

Instruments with faces. A cast of illustrated characters, a guitar, a saxophone, a trumpet, a keyboard, carries the identity, set against a chunky mix of checkerboards, stripes and clashing brights. Two logo lockups, a curved badge and a stacked wordmark, let the mark flex between wayfinding and hero moments.

What we made

Mood boards, brand concept, a full asset library, application samples and a brand book. The system was then rolled out into welcome walls, wayfinding, photo-booth backdrops, lobby and elevator screens, menus, a save-the-date animation and a splash page.
