Prompted Playlist

The press-briefing deck for Prompted Playlist, Spotify's feature that turns a sentence into a personalised playlist.

Year
2026
Services
Deck design, Visual system, Product storytelling

The brief

Ahead of the January 2026 launch, Spotify's communications team needed a deck for virtual briefings with press, led by the VP of Product Personalization and the Head of Global Music Curation and Discovery. It had to explain a new product clearly, carry embargo rules, and look like a launch, not a status update.

The idea

The product is about taste made visible, so the slides are built from spray-textured, hand-made patterns in acid green, pink and yellow that shift from slide to slide. Device mock-ups sit inside the pattern rather than on top of it, and the three inputs that shape a prompted playlist, genre, mood and world knowledge, become the closing visual.

What we made

Style discovery in two rounds, then full design across 21 slides including speakers, embargo, product walkthrough, example prompts and Q&A. Delivered as an editable template so the team could drop in final brand and creative assets as they landed.
